# Data Retention Schedule (v3)
Customer transaction records: retain 7 years (regulatory). Support tickets: 3 years.
Application logs containing no personal data: 18 months. Logs containing personal data:
90 days, after which they must be purged or anonymized. Email: 5 years for Finance and
Legal staff, 2 years for all others. Backups follow the source system's schedule plus 30
days. Legal hold suspends every schedule above — deletion during a hold is a reportable
compliance event. The retention system of record is the Data Governance Registry, owned
by the Data Governance Council, which meets monthly and is chaired by the CISO.
