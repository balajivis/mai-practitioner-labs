# Travel & Expense Policy (v4, effective 2026-03-01)
All business travel must be booked through the Meridian Travel Portal. Economy class is
standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is permitted beyond that.
Any single flight over $800 requires written approval from the traveler's VP before booking.
International trips additionally require a Trip Brief filed 10 business days in advance.
Meal reimbursement is capped at $75/day domestic and $110/day international, receipts required
above $25. Ride-sharing is preferred over rental cars in cities where Meridian has no office.
Expense reports are due within 15 days of return; late reports route to the cost-center owner
for exception approval. The Finance owner for travel exceptions is the Controller's office.
