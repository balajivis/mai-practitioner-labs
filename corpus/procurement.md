# Procurement & Vendor Policy (v2)
Purchases under $5,000 use the corporate card with manager approval. From $5,000 to
$50,000, three competitive quotes and a purchase order are required. Above $50,000,
Procurement runs a formal RFP and Legal reviews the contract. All software purchases,
regardless of amount, additionally require a security review by the Vendor Risk team —
this includes free tools that process company data. New vendors must pass sanctions
screening before first payment. Invoice terms default to net-45; exceptions to net-30
need CFO sign-off. The preferred-vendor list is maintained by Procurement in VendorHub.
