# Travel & Expense Policy (v2, SUPERSEDED 2026-03-01 — retained for audit)
NOTE: This version is retained for historical audit only. See v4 for current rules.
Under v2, flights over $500 required VP approval, meal reimbursement was capped at
$60/day domestic, and expense reports were due within 30 days of return. Booking outside
the portal was permitted with receipts. This version ceased to apply on 2026-03-01.
