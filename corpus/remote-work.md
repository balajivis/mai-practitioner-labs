# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy (v5)
Meridian is hybrid-first: teams anchor on Tuesday–Thursday in-office; Monday and Friday
are flexible. Fully remote arrangements require director approval and an annual re-review.
Remote employees must work from their registered country of employment; working abroad
beyond 30 days per year requires prior approval from both HR and Tax (via the Global
Mobility desk). Home-office equipment stipend is $600 on joining and $250/year after.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 in each team's anchor timezone. Customer-facing
teams follow their own coverage rosters, which take precedence over anchor days.
